# Identity

- arXiv: 0812.3560 (v3); Fortschritte der Physik 57 (2009) 907-1036. PhD thesis, Bonn.
- Title: Towards Low Energy Physics from the Heterotic String. Author: Saúl Ramos-Sánchez. 160 PDF pages.
- Read: Sec. 2.2 (compactification on orbifolds, the Z_3 orbifold as standard example, Wilson lines, pp. 13-27) and
  Appendix D, Tables D.3-D.4 (pp. 126-128).

# Structure

Ch. 2: heterotic string (2.1), orbifolds (2.2: space groups, fixed points, the Z_3 orbifold on the SU(3)^3 lattice with
twist v = (0, 1/3, 1/3, -2/3), 27 fixed points, Wilson lines as embeddings of lattice translations and their order
conditions, modular invariance), strings on orbifolds (2.3), discrete torsion (2.4), Yukawa couplings (2.5). Ch. 3:
classification of orbifolds. Chs. 4-6: MSSM searches. App. D: tables.

# Sections and equations used (part-vi-02-compactifications, sec-compactification-fermions)

- Sec. 2.2, "Standard Example: The Z_3 Orbifold" (pp. 24-25, eq. 2.60): twist (1/3, 1/3, -2/3), 27 fixed points, the
  theta^2 sector has the same fixed points as theta and contains the antiparticles: prp-cff-orbifold-fermions,
  exm-cff-twisted, exr-cff-fixed-points.
- Sec. 2.2 (Wilson lines, pp. 19-21): Wilson lines are the embeddings of the lattice generators, of order 3 on the Z_3
  orbifold: exm-cff-twisted.
- Appendix D, Table D.4 (p. 127): standard embedding V = (1/3, 1/3, -2/3, 0^5)(0^8), gauge group E6 x SU(3) x E8,
  untwisted 3 (27, 3, 1), twisted 27 x (27, 1, 1) + 27 x 3 triplets of SU(3): prp-cff-orbifold-fermions, exm-cff-twisted.

# Caveats

- The text extraction of Table D.4 loses overlines; the triplets of the twisted sector are quoted without conjugation.
- The twisted sector is a string-theory result; the tutorial derives only the untwisted sector in field theory
  (exm-cff-z3-phases, exr-cff-z3-projection).
