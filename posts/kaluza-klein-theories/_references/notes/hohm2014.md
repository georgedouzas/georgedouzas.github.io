# Conventions

- Setting: exceptional field theory (EFT), the manifestly U-duality covariant formulation of untruncated 11d and IIB supergravity. Coordinates $(x^\mu, Y^M)$: $x^\mu$ "external", $Y^M$ "internal" generalized coordinates, $M,N$ in the fundamental of $E_{d(d)}$, $2 \le d \le 8$ (Intro, p. 2). Detailed treatment uses $E_{7(7)}$ EFT: four $x^\mu$, $\mu=0,\dots,3$, and 56 internal $Y^M$, $M=1,\dots,56$ (Sec. 2, p. 6).
- Section condition (eq. (2.1), p. 6): $(t_\alpha)^{MN}\,\partial_M\otimes\partial_N \equiv 0$, $\Omega^{MN}\,\partial_M\otimes\partial_N \equiv 0$, where $\otimes$ means the derivatives may act on different fields, $\Omega^{MN}$ is the symplectic invariant (used to raise/lower: $X^M=\Omega^{MN}X_N$, $X_N = X^M\Omega_{MN}$), and $(t_\alpha)_M{}^N$, $\alpha=1,\dots,133$, are the $E_{7(7)}$ generators. The strong form is imposed throughout (p. 5, p. 31).
- Two inequivalent solutions of (2.1): fields depend on 7 or 6 of the 56 coordinates, giving $D=11$ supergravity or type IIB, respectively (eq. (2.34), p. 11: $\mathbf{56}\to \boxed{7_{+3}} + 21'_{+1} + 21_{-1} + 7'_{-3}$ under GL(7); $\mathbf{56}\to \boxed{(6,1)_{+2}} + (6',2)_{+1} + (20,1)_0 + (6,2)_{-1} + (6',1)_{-2}$ under GL(6) x SL(2)).
- $E_{7(7)}$ EFT field content (eq. (2.2), p. 7): $\{e_\mu{}^\alpha, \mathcal{M}_{MN}, \mathcal{A}_\mu{}^M, \mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu\,\alpha}, \mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu\,M}\}$, with $\mathcal{M}_{MN}=(\mathcal{V}\mathcal{V}^T)_{MN}$ parametrizing $E_{7(7)}/SU(8)$. $\mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu M}$ is a "covariantly constrained" 2-form obeying (2.3) (same algebraic constraints as (2.1)).
- Generalized diffeomorphisms (eq. (2.4), p. 7): $\mathbb{L}_\Lambda \equiv \Lambda^K\partial_K + 12\,\partial_K\Lambda^L (t^\alpha)_L{}^K t_\alpha + \lambda\,\partial_P\Lambda^P$ on tensors of weight $\lambda$; weights (eq. (2.5)): $e_\mu{}^\alpha$: 1/2, $\mathcal{M}_{MN}$: 0, $\mathcal{A}_\mu{}^M$: 1/2, $\mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu\alpha}$: 1, $\mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu M}$: 1/2.
- Lagrangian (eq. (2.8), p. 8): $\mathcal{L}_{\rm EFT} = e\widehat R + \tfrac{1}{48} e g^{\mu\nu}\mathcal{D}_\mu\mathcal{M}^{MN}\mathcal{D}_\nu\mathcal{M}_{MN} - \tfrac18 e\mathcal{M}_{MN}\mathcal{F}^{\mu\nu M}\mathcal{F}_{\mu\nu}{}^N + \mathcal{L}_{\rm top} - eV(\mathcal{M}_{MN},g_{\mu\nu})$, with potential (2.12).
- Normalization caveat (footnote 2, p. 12): relative to the general form (1.2), the $E_{7(7)}$ equations (3.2) carry an explicit factor 1/7; vectors, 2-forms and the embedding tensor are rescaled relative to de Wit-Samtleben-Trigiante [51,52]: $\sqrt2 A_\mu{}^M_{[1103.2785]} = A_\mu{}^M_{\rm [here]}$, $\Theta_{M}{}^\alpha{}_{[1103.2785]} = \sqrt2\,\Theta_M{}^\alpha{}_{\rm [here]}$, $\vartheta_M^{[1103.2785]} = \sqrt2\,\vartheta_M^{\rm [here]}$.

# Equations the tutorial uses

(a) Generalized Scherk-Schwarz ansatz.

- Scalars, eq. (1.1) p. 3 = eq. (3.1) p. 12: $\mathcal{M}_{MN}(x,Y) = U_M{}^{\underline K}(Y)\,U_N{}^{\underline L}(Y)\,M_{\underline{KL}}(x)$, with $U$ an $E_{7(7)}$-valued twist matrix; underlined indices refer to the global $E_{7(7)}$ preserved by the ansatz (p. 13).
- Remaining fields, eq. (3.5) p. 13 (cf. (1.3) p. 3):
  $e_\mu{}^\alpha(x,Y) = \rho^{-1}(Y)\,e_\mu{}^\alpha(x)$,
  $\mathcal{A}_\mu{}^M(x,Y) = A_\mu{}^N(x)(U^{-1})_N{}^M(Y)\,\rho^{-1}(Y)$,
  $\mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu\,\alpha}(x,Y) = \rho^{-2}(Y)\,U_\alpha{}^\beta(Y)\,B_{\mu\nu\,\beta}(x)$,
  with $U_\alpha{}^\beta$ the twist in the adjoint. Rule (p. 13): every field is rotated by $U$ on its $E_{7(7)}$ indices and scaled by $\rho^{-2\lambda}$, $\lambda$ its weight in (2.5); in general $D$ the factor is $\rho^{-(D-2)\lambda}$ (p. 15).
- Gauge parameter, eq. (3.3) p. 13: $\Lambda^M(x,Y) = \rho^{-1}(Y)(U^{-1})_P{}^M(Y)\Lambda^P(x)$.
- Constrained compensator (non-standard), eq. (3.8) p. 14 = (1.4) p. 3: $\mathcal{B}_{\mu\nu\,M} = -2\rho^{-2}(U^{-1})_S{}^P\,\partial_M U_P{}^R\,(t^\alpha)_R{}^S\,B_{\mu\nu\,\alpha}$.
- Fermions, eq. (3.25) p. 17: $\psi_\mu{}^i(x,Y) = \rho^{-1/2}(Y)\psi_\mu{}^i(x)$, $\chi^{ijk}(x,Y) = \rho^{1/2}(Y)\chi^{ijk}(x)$ (weights (2.31)-(2.32): $\lambda(\psi)=\lambda(\epsilon)=1/4$, $\lambda(\chi)=-1/4$). Footnote 3: the ansatz carries no Killing spinors of the internal manifold.

(b) Consistency conditions (embedding tensor constant).

- $E_{7(7)}$ form, eq. (3.2) p. 12:
  $\big[(U^{-1})_{\underline M}{}^P (U^{-1})_{\underline N}{}^Q\,\partial_P U_Q{}^{\underline K}\big]_{(912)} \overset{!}{=} \tfrac17\,\rho\,\Theta_{\underline M}{}^\alpha (t_\alpha)_{\underline N}{}^{\underline K}$,
  $\partial_N (U^{-1})_{\underline M}{}^N - 3\rho^{-1}\partial_N\rho\,(U^{-1})_{\underline M}{}^N \overset{!}{=} 2\rho\,\vartheta_{\underline M}$,
  with $\rho(Y)$ a scale factor and $\Theta_M{}^\alpha$, $\vartheta_M$ constant (the embedding tensor and the trombone embedding tensor); $[\cdot]_{(912)}$ projects onto the $\mathbf{912}$.
- General-$D$ form, eq. (3.10) p. 14 = (1.2) p. 3:
  $\big[(U^{-1})_M{}^P (U^{-1})_N{}^L\,\partial_P U_L{}^K\big]_{(\mathbb{P})} \overset{!}{=} \rho\,\Theta_M{}^\alpha (t_\alpha)_N{}^K$,
  $\partial_N (U^{-1})_M{}^N - (D-1)\rho^{-1}\partial_N\rho\,(U^{-1})_M{}^N \overset{!}{=} \rho\,(D-2)\,\vartheta_M$,
  $(\mathbb{P})$ = projection onto the embedding-tensor representation of the $D$-dimensional gauged supergravity.
- Equivalent form, eqs. (3.11)-(3.12) p. 14: $[\mathcal{X}_M{}^\alpha]_{(\mathbb P)} \overset{!}{=} \rho\Theta_M{}^\alpha$, $\mathcal{X}_{KM}{}^K \overset{!}{=} (1-D)\rho^{-1}\partial_N\rho\,(U^{-1})_M{}^N + \rho(2-D)\vartheta_M$, where $\mathcal{X}_{MN}{}^K \equiv (U^{-1})_M{}^P(U^{-1})_N{}^L\partial_P U_L{}^K \equiv \mathcal{X}_M{}^\alpha (t_\alpha)_N{}^K$.
- Induced gauge action on $M_{MN}(x)$, eq. (3.4) p. 13: $\delta_\Lambda M_{MN}(x) = 2\Lambda^L(x)\big(\Theta_L{}^\alpha + 8\vartheta_R (t^\alpha)_L{}^R\big)(t_\alpha)_{(M}{}^P M_{N)P}(x)$; (3.2) plus section condition imply the quadratic constraints of [51,52] (p. 13; footnote 4 p. 19).
- Trombone: $\vartheta_M\neq0$ gives a consistent truncation at the level of equations of motion only, no lower-dimensional action; $\vartheta_M=0$ needed for consistency at the level of the action (Sec. 3.2, pp. 15-16, eqs. (3.13)-(3.15)).
- SL(n) specialization, eq. (4.5) p. 20 with $\Theta_{AB,C}{}^D = \delta^D_{[A}\eta_{B]C} + Z_{ABC}{}^D$ (4.4); with $Z=0=\vartheta$ (4.10), eqs. (4.11a-c) p. 20:
  $\partial_{CD}(U^{-1})_{(A}{}^C (U^{-1})_{B)}{}^D \overset{!}{=} \tfrac12(1-n)\rho\,\eta_{AB}$,
  $\partial_{CD}\big[\rho^{1-D}(U^{-1})_{AB}{}^{CD}\big] \overset{!}{=} 0$,
  $(U^{-1})_{ABC}{}^{GHE}\partial_{GH}U_E{}^D \overset{!}{=} \tfrac{D-1}{n-2}(U^{-1})_{[AB}{}^{GH}\delta_{C]}{}^D\rho^{-1}\partial_{GH}\rho$.
  Section-condition solution: $\partial_{ij}U_A{}^B = 0$, $i,j=1,\dots,n-1$, i.e. $U$ depends only on $y^i\equiv Y^{0i}$ (eq. (4.14) p. 21).

(c) Which reductions and which gauged supergravities.

- Integer relation, eq. (1.6) p. 4 = (4.28) p. 23: $\tfrac12(D-1) = \tfrac{n-2}{n-4}$, with principal solutions (4.29): $(D,n) = (7,5), (5,6), (4,8)$, i.e. SL(5), $E_{6(6)}$, $E_{7(7)}$ EFT -- "the dimensions of the known sphere compactifications $\mathrm{AdS}_D\times S^{n-1}$".
- $\eta_{AB} = \mathrm{diag}(\underbrace{1,\dots}_{p},\underbrace{-1,\dots}_{q},\underbrace{0,\dots}_{r})$, $p+q+r=n$ (4.12) p. 21; gauge group CSO(p,q,r), = SO(p,q) for r=0.
- Explicit SO(p,q) twist, eq. (4.18) p. 22: $(U^{-1})_0{}^0 = (1-v)^{(n-1)/n}$, $(U^{-1})_0{}^i = \eta_{ij}y^j(1-v)^{(n-2)/(2n)}K(u,v)$, $(U^{-1})_i{}^0 = \eta_{ij}y^j(1-v)^{(n-2)/(2n)}$, $(U^{-1})_i{}^j = (1-v)^{-1/n}(\delta^{ij} + \eta_{ik}\eta_{jl}y^ky^lK(u,v))$, $u\equiv y^iy^i$, $v\equiv y^i\eta_{ij}y^j$; $K$ solves ODE (4.19) (hypergeometric solutions (4.23), (4.25)); $\rho = \rho_{p,q} = (1-v)^{(n-4)/(2n)}$ (4.26). CSO(p,q,r) extension via block form (4.30) with $\beta = (1-v)^{-1/((p+q)(p+q+r))}$ (4.36), $\rho = \beta^{-2r}\rho_{p,q}$ (4.31).
- Embedding into $E_{6(6)}$ and $E_{7(7)}$ via SL(6) ⊂ $E_{6(6)}$, SL(8) ⊂ $E_{7(7)}$ (4.37), twist matrices (4.44) p. 25 and (4.52) p. 27; the ansatz for $E_{6(6)}$ written out in (4.45) p. 26.
- Results: $D=7$ CSO(p,q,r), $p+q+r=5$, from $D=11$ (p. 24); $D=5$ CSO(p,q,r), $p+q+r=6$, from IIB (p. 26); $D=4$ CSO(p,q,r), $p+q+r=8$, from $D=11$ (p. 28). The three maximally supersymmetric cases are $\mathrm{AdS}_7\times S^4$, $\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5$, $\mathrm{AdS}_4\times S^7$ (p. 5).
- Induced internal metric, eq. (1.7) p. 5 = (4.61) p. 29: $ds^2 = (1+u-v)^{-2/(p+q+r-2)}\big(dy^z dy^z + dy^a dy^b(\delta_{ab} + \tfrac{\eta_{ac}\eta_{bd}y^cy^d}{1-v})\big)$, conformal to $H^{p,q}\times\mathbb{R}^r$ (4.62), $H^{p,q}$: $y^A\eta_{AB}y^B = 1$ (4.63); round sphere for $r=q=0$; for $p+q+r=8$ it is the Hull-Warner warped hyperboloid [53].
- Footnote 1 p. 5: the theory is IIA for $D=4,7$ and IIB for $D=5$.

(d) Why this proves consistency. Section 3.2 p. 19 summary: "the generalized Scherk-Schwarz ansatz is consistent in the full exceptional field theory and exactly reproduces all field equations and transformation rules of the lower-dimensional gauged supergravity, provided the twist matrices satisfy (3.2) and the section condition (2.1)." Mechanism (p. 15): all covariant derivatives and field strengths reduce "covariantly" -- $E_{7(7)}$ indices are simply rotated by $U$ up to an overall power of $\rho$ -- so all $Y$-dependence factors out (eqs. (3.7), (3.9), (3.14), (3.16)-(3.18), (3.28)-(3.29), (3.33)). Sec. 4.4 p. 28: "consistency of the truncation follows from the general structure of the ansatz and the covariant formulation of exceptional field theory". The dictionary EFT ↔ 11d/IIB is independent of the twist, so a consistent EFT truncation is a consistent truncation of the original supergravity (pp. 5, 28, 30).

(e) Relation to ordinary Scherk-Schwarz. (i) $\rho$ "has no analogue in the standard Scherk-Schwarz reduction [15]" but ensures covariance of (3.2) under generalized diffeomorphisms (p. 12). (ii) The trombone condition $\vartheta_M=0$ "is the analogue of the unimodularity condition $f_{MN}{}^M=0$ to be imposed in standard Scherk-Schwarz reductions [15] for invariance of the measure"; nonzero $f_{MN}{}^M\equiv\vartheta_N$ does not spoil consistency, only the existence of an action (p. 16). (iii) Existence: "For conventional Scherk-Schwarz compactifications the existence of proper twist matrices is guaranteed by Lie's second theorem, but to our knowledge there is no corresponding theorem in this generalized context" (p. 4; repeated p. 30). (iv) Naive exponentiation $Y^M\Theta_M{}^\alpha t_\alpha$ fails: Jacobi identities fail for the "structure constants" $\Theta_M{}^\alpha(t_\alpha)_N{}^K$, the projection in (1.2) is non-trivial, and such an object generally violates the section constraint; hence "the standard sphere compactifications take a highly 'non-geometric' form" (pp. 5-6).

# Attributable statements

1. Consistent truncation defined: any solution of the lower-dimensional theory embeds into a solution of the higher-dimensional one; all internal coordinate dependence factors out (Intro, p. 2).
2. Toroidal consistency follows because retained fields are U(1)^n singlets; sphere consistency was so non-trivial it "shed serious doubt" early on; $S^7$ established in de Wit-Nicolai [6] (Intro, p. 2).
3. The generalized Scherk-Schwarz ansatz (1.1)/(3.1) with twist equations (1.2)/(3.10) reduces the full EFT field equations precisely to those of gauged supergravity with embedding tensor $\Theta_M{}^\alpha$, $\vartheta_M$ (abstract; result (1), pp. 3-4; Sec. 3.2 p. 19).
4. Consistency of the constrained compensator requires the non-standard ansatz (3.8)/(1.4) (result (1) p. 3-4; p. 14).
5. The fermionic ansatz is "remarkably simple": $Y$-dependence is a power of $\rho$ only, with no Killing spinors (p. 4, eq. (3.25), footnote 3 p. 17).
6. Explicit SL(n) twists solve the consistency conditions for CSO(p,q,r) with $p+q+r=n$, provided $\tfrac12(D-1)=\tfrac{n-2}{n-4}$, integer solutions $(7,5),(5,6),(4,8)$ (eqs. (1.6), (4.28)-(4.29), pp. 4, 23).
7. The construction extends generalized Scherk-Schwarz beyond homogeneous spaces (warped hyperboloids $H^{p,q}$, as conjectured by Hull-Warner [53]) (abstract; p. 4; Sec. 4.4).
8. The induced metric (4.61) is in general not a solution of the higher-dimensional equations; this matches the absence of a critical point at the origin of the scalar potential, yet the truncation remains consistent (pp. 5, 29). For SO(p,8-p) only $(8,0),(4,4),(5,3)$ admit a Freund-Rubin ansatz [53] (p. 29).
9. Strong section constraint imposed throughout; twist and $\rho$ depend only on the usual supergravity coordinates (pp. 5, 31). Intriguingly, the match with gauged supergravity "does not explicitly use the section constraint" (Conclusions, p. 31).
10. Trombone gauging $\vartheta_M\neq0$: consistent at the level of equations of motion, no action (pp. 4, 15-16).

# Caveats

- Normalizations: (3.2) has an explicit 1/7 relative to (3.10)/(1.2); fields and embedding tensor rescaled by $\sqrt2$ relative to [51,52] (footnote 2 p. 12). Do not mix (3.2) and (3.10) coefficients.
- Underlined vs bare indices in (3.1)-(3.2): underlined = global $E_{7(7)}$ of the embedding tensor formalism; the paper drops the distinction after p. 13.
- The internal metric is read off from $\mathcal{M}^{MN}$ via $\mathcal{M}^{i0,j0} = g^{(4-n)/n}g^{ij}$ (4.58), and (4.59)-(4.61) hold at the "origin" $M_{PQ}=\delta_{PQ}$ only; the general uplift requires the full EFT dictionary [45,46], which is independent of the twist (pp. 28-29).
- Trombone $\vartheta_M=0$ and $Z_{ABC}{}^D=0$ are imposed for the explicit sphere/hyperboloid solutions (4.10); more general twists (e.g. $D=7$ SO(4) gauging with $\eta=0$, the $S^4$ reduction of IIA via SO(5,5) EFT, non-maximal $\mathrm{AdS}_3\times S^3$) are left open (Conclusions pp. 30-31).
- For CSO(p,q,r) with $r>0$ the relation (4.28) is not forced: (4.34) allows other $D$; the paper restricts to (4.35) (p. 24).
- Quadratic constraints on $\Theta$ are automatic from (3.2) plus the section condition (p. 13, footnote 4 p. 19); they are used in the potential computation (3.33).
- The full SL(5) EFT (with $D=7$ metric and forms) had not been constructed at the time; only the internal sector is used in Sec. 4.1 (p. 19).
