# Identity

- arXiv: 0910.5464 (v1); Fortschritte der Physik 58 (2010) 467-502.
- Title: A Three-Generation Calabi-Yau Manifold with Small Hodge Numbers. Authors: Volker Braun, Philip Candelas, Rhys
  Davies. 50 PDF pages.
- Read: abstract, Sec. 1 (introduction, pp. 1-3), Sec. 3 opening and 3.1 (pp. 15-17).

# Structure

Sec. 1: the manifold Y with chi = -72, free actions of Z_12 and Dic_3, quotients with chi = -6 and (h11, h21) = (1, 4);
standard embedding gives E_6 with 3 net generations; Hosotani mechanism; conifold transition to (2, 2); comparison with
the Yau manifold (6, 9) and a manifold (5, 8); Figure 1 (tip of the distribution). Sec. 2: construction of Y. Sec. 3:
phenomenology: E6 x E8 with four 27 and one 27bar; deformation of T + O to SU(4) bundles (Spin(10)); Wilson lines
(SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) x U(1)' or SU(4) x SU(2) x U(1)). Secs. 4-7: symmetries, conifolds, toric mirror, Z_12 quotient.

# Caveats

- The paper uses the counting 27 <-> h21 for its quotient; the general statement for the standard embedding
  (H^1(T) ~ H^{2,1}, H^1(T*) ~ H^{1,1}) is the Dolbeault count of exm-spd-generations-quintic.
