# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

The circle is y in [-L, L], circumference 2L; the tutorial writes ell = 2L. The metric of Sec. 2.1 is a mostly-plus
parametrisation with the radion phi; at phi = 1 the potentials reduce to the vacuum energies used here.
