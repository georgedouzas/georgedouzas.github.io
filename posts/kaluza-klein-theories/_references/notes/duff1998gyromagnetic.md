# Identity

- arXiv: hep-th/9801072 (v1); Nuclear Physics B 524 (1998) 129-140.
- Title: g=1 for Dirichlet 0-branes. Authors: M. J. Duff, James T. Liu, J. Rahmfeld. 13 PDF pages.
- Read: Secs. 1 and 2 (pp. 1-4); Sec. 3 skimmed.

# Structure

Sec. 1: four descriptions of D0-branes, among them the massive Kaluza-Klein modes of D = 11 supergravity on a circle,
with M_n = |n|/R and Q_n = n. Sec. 2: g = 1 from Kaluza-Klein arguments: the result of Hosoya et al. [15] that all massive
KK states of a 5 -> 4 reduction share g = 1; why this does not contradict the unitarity argument for g = 2; the
generalisation of g to any dimension, eqs. (1)-(2); the reduction of a d-form, eqs. (3)-(6); the string-theory formula
(7)-(8). Sec. 3: g = 1 from the black-hole (supergravity soliton) side.

# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

Kaluza-Klein metric with e^{2 phi} (eq. 3), mostly-plus signature; only linear order in the KK gauge field is used.
The tutorial derives g = 1 for the spinor from its own reduction (prp-kkf-mode-equation) and cites this paper for the
general statement and for the unitarity argument.

# Sections and equations used (part-iii-02-gauge-tower, sec-kk-fermions)

- Sec. 2 (first paragraph): all massive KK states have g = 1; tree-level unitarity requires g = 2 only for
  M_Pl > E > M/Q, an empty range for KK states: prp-kkf-mode-equation, exm-kkf-g-factor, section introduction.
- Sec. 2, eqs. (1)-(5) and the sentence "Similar arguments may be used to show that g = 1 holds for the reduction of
  arbitrary fields": exm-kkf-g-factor.

# Caveats

- The paper does not treat the spinor explicitly; it treats d-form potentials and states the general result.
