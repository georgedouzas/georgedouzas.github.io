# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

Signature (+, -, -, -), D = d/dx - i e_0 A_0 (charge sign opposite to ours), d = 4 - 2 epsilon: Grozin's 1/epsilon is our 2/epsilon,
so Z = 1 + z alpha/(4 pi epsilon_G) becomes 1 + 2 z alpha/(4 pi epsilon). His MS includes e^(-gamma epsilon) in the definition of alpha
(eq. 3.9), which is our modified minimal scheme. His anomalous dimensions are d log Z/d log mu without the factor 1/2.
