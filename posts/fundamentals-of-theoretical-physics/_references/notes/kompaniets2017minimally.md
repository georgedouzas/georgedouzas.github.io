# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

D = 4 - 2 epsilon_KP, so the tutorial's epsilon = 4 - d is 2 epsilon_KP; g = lambda/(16 pi^2) for the interaction (lambda/4!)(phi.phi)^2.
Their gamma_m2 is the derivative of log Z_m2 and has the opposite sign of the tutorial's mu d log m^2/d mu; their nu =
[2 + gamma_m2(g*)]^-1 is the tutorial's (2 - gamma_m2)^-1.
