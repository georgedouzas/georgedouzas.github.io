# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

Euclidean throughout; D = -(g^mu nu nabla_mu nabla_nu + E); curvature sign with R = 2 on the unit sphere, as in the tutorial.
Heat coefficients a_k with K(t) ~ sum t^((k - n)/2) a_k; odd a_k vanish on manifolds without boundary.
