# Identity

- arXiv: hep-ph/9503416 (v1, 23 Mar 1995); American Journal of Physics 63 (1995) 1119.
- Title: The Foldy-Wouthuysen transformation. Authors: John P. Costella, Bruce H. J. McKellar. 6 PDF pages, read in full.

# Structure

I introduction; II Newton and Wigner (localised states, position operator); III Foldy and Wouthuysen: eq. (1) H_NW = beta
sqrt(m^2 + p^2), eq. (2) velocity beta p/W_p, the power-series transformation and its validity; IV the Dirac-Pauli representation
and minimal coupling; V the two faces of the electron (magnetic moment and Darwin "charge radius").

# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

hbar = c = 1; their Dirac-Pauli representation is the tutorial's Dirac representation; "odd" and "even" as in def-dq-even-odd.
