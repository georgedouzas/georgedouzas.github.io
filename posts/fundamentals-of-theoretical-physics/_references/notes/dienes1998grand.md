# Identity

- arXiv: hep-ph/9806292 (v2); Nuclear Physics B 537 (1999) 47-108.
- Title: Grand Unification at Intermediate Mass Scales through Extra Dimensions. Authors: Keith R. Dienes, Emilian Dudas,
  Tony Gherghetta. Read: Sections 1-3 (introduction, the higher-dimensional MSSM, renormalisability and cutoffs, gauge
  coupling unification) and Appendix A (the vacuum polarisation with Kaluza-Klein towers).

# Structure

Sec. 2: the higher-dimensional MSSM on orbifolds, eqs. 2.11-2.12 (dimension of the D-dimensional coupling, alpha =
R^-delta alpha-tilde); Sec. 2.2: non-renormalisability, the coupling depends on the cutoff, truncated towers as a
renormalisable equivalent. Sec. 3: eqs. 3.1-3.6 (MSSM running, b = (11, 1, -3) for (Y, 2, 3), alpha^-1(M_Z) values, M_GUT);
eq. 3.8 (Pi(0) with the tower), eq. 3.9 (b-tilde = (3/5, -3, -6) + eta(4, 4, 4)), eq. 3.10 (X_delta = pi^(delta/2)/Gamma(1 +
delta/2)), eqs. 3.11-3.13 (one-loop coupling as a function of the cutoff), eqs. 3.14-3.16 (ratios B_ij). Appendix A:
eqs. A.1-A.9 (vacuum polarisation of a Dirac fermion with its tower, proper time), A.10-A.13 (theta function), A.14-A.15
(cutoffs with r = pi X_delta^(-2/delta)).

# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

alpha_1 = (5/3) alpha_Y, b_1 = (3/5) b_Y; mu_0 = 1/R; delta = D - 4. The metric signature plays no role in the results used.
