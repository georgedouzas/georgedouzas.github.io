# Exercises in Analysis

Leszek Gasiński and Nikolaos S. Papageorgiou, Springer (Problem Books in Mathematics), 2014. Local copy: `references/textbooks/Gasińksi and Papageorgiou - 2014 - Exercises in Analysis Part 1.pdf` (git-ignored). Textbook (design D32), from the author's Zotero library, which was copied in full
for change kk-csdr-physics-foundations and later changes (design E5).

## Chapters it serves

About 1,016 problems (Ch. 1: 210, Ch. 2: 218, Ch. 3: 202, Ch. 4: 207, Ch. 5: 179), each with a complete worked
solution. It is a primary source of proof exercises for the Part I backfill of change kk-csdr-ten-examples (design X5).
