# Conventions

Inner products linear in the first slot in Ch. 2, but the results used here do not depend on it. Banach spaces over
$\mathbb{C}$; $L(X, Y)$ for bounded operators, $X^*$ for the dual, $T'$ for the Banach-space transpose.
