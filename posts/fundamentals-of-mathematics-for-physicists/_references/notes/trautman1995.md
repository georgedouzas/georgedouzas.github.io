# Identity

- arXiv: hep-th/9810018 (v1, 2 Oct 1998); published Acta Physica Polonica B 26 (1995) 1283-1310 (stated on the PDF).
- Title: The Dirac operator on hypersurfaces. Author: Andrzej Trautman (Warsaw; SISSA Trieste). Printed page numbers 1283-1310.

# Structure

1. Introduction. 2. Notation and preliminaries: 2.1 Clifford algebras and pin groups (Pin, Spin, volume element) [read];
2.2 bundles. 3. Representations of the pin groups: 3.1 vector representations (twisted adjoint rho, eqs. (2)-(5), Prop. 1)
[read]; 3.2 spinor representations (Prop. 2: Dirac, Weyl, Pauli and Cartan spinors; Prop. 3) [read]; 3.3 extension from m to
m + 1 (Prop. 4). 4. Pin structures and bundles of spinors: 4.1 definitions (eqs. (13)-(14), Stiefel-Whitney classes) [read];
4.2 triviality of associated bundles (Prop. 5); 4.3 spheres and real projective spaces (RP^{4k+1} not spin, RP^{4k-1} two spin
structures) [read]. 5. The modified Dirac operator. 6. Pin structures on hypersurfaces (6.1: spin structures of the circle from
immersions z -> z^n) [read]. 7. Schroedinger transform of the Dirac operator. 8. Applications: 8.1 spectrum of S^m (Prop. 8)
[read]; 8.2 real projective spaces (Prop. 9) [read].

# Conventions and mapping to the tutorial

- Clifford algebra with v^2 = h(v, v) for a quadratic form of signature (k, l): the tutorial's Cl(p, q) with p = k.
- Twisted adjoint representation rho(a) v = alpha(a) v a^{-1}; on Spin it is conjugation, the tutorial's rho(S) v = S v S^{-1}.
- Prop. 8 gives the spectrum +-(l + m/2) with multiplicity 2^nu binom(m + l - 1, l); Prop. 9 the spectrum {-1/2 +- (2l + m/2 +
  1/2)} of RP^m with multiplicity 2^nu binom(|lambda| + m/2 - 1, |lambda| - m/2) (read as such; the tutorial checks it for m = 3).
